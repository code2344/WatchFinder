# WatchFinder ⌚

Find your perfect watch in 10 questions! A comprehensive watch recommendation quiz with accurate data from 1,975+ watches including smart watches, analog timepieces, and hybrid models.

![WatchFinder Preview](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/263ffc78-4ad7-47d6-8855-460e8240d1b8)

## Features

- 🎯 **10 Comprehensive Questions** covering budget, type, use case, battery life, connectivity, features, water resistance, display, brand preference, and size
- 💎 **1,975+ Watches** with accurate specifications researched from manufacturer data
- 🔍 **Smart Matching Algorithm** that evaluates watches based on your preferences
- 📊 **Top 3 Recommendations** with detailed specifications and match percentage
- 📱 **Responsive Design** works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- ⚡ **Fast & Lightweight** - pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (no frameworks required)

## Quiz Questions

1. **Budget** - Choose from under $300, $300-$1,000, $1,000-$3,000, or over $3,000
2. **Watch Type** - Smart, Analog/Mechanical, Hybrid, or no preference
3. **Primary Use** - Sports, Fitness, Outdoor, Daily Wear, Business, or Formal
4. **Battery Life** - Daily charging, weekly, monthly, or never (automatic/solar)
5. **Connectivity** - Essential (LTE/WiFi/Bluetooth), Basic (Bluetooth), Optional, or None
6. **Key Features** - Health monitoring, GPS, Fitness tracking, Durability, Style, or Basic time
7. **Water Resistance** - Basic splash (30-50m), Swimming (100m), Diving (200m+), Professional (300m+)
8. **Display Type** - Analog, Digital, AMOLED, Hybrid, or no preference
9. **Brand Preference** - Apple, Samsung, Garmin, Luxury Swiss, Japanese, or any
10. **Size** - Small (<40mm), Medium (40-44mm), Large (45mm+), or no preference

## Matching Algorithm

The quiz uses a sophisticated scoring system with weighted factors:
- **Budget Match** (30 points) - Prioritizes watches within your price range
- **Watch Type** (25 points) - Smart vs Analog vs Hybrid preference
- **Use Case** (20 points) - Matches watch capabilities to your intended use
- **Battery Life** (15 points) - Ensures battery meets your expectations
- **Features** (15 points) - Matches key features you care about
- **Brand** (15 points) - Considers brand preferences
- **Connectivity** (10 points) - Bluetooth, WiFi, LTE requirements
- **Water Resistance** (10 points) - Matches water resistance needs
- **Display** (10 points) - Preferred display technology
- **Size** (5 points) - Watch size preference

Total possible score: 145 points. The top 3 watches with the highest match percentage are recommended.

## How to Use

1. **Open `index.html`** in any modern web browser
2. **Click "Start Quiz"** to begin
3. **Answer 10 questions** about your watch preferences
4. **Review your top 3 matches** with detailed specifications and pricing
5. **Take the quiz again** to explore different options
